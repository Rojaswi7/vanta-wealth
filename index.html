    Security

    Read-only by design.

    Vanta can see your accounts. It can never move money out of them.

    Read-only connections

    Aggregation uses view-only credentials. Trading and transfers stay with your custodian.

    Hardware-key sign-in

    Passkeys or a physical security key for every household member. No SMS codes.

    Privacy mode

    Blur every balance with one keystroke when you open the app in a meeting or on a plane.

    Scoped sharing

    Grant your CPA the tax lots, your trustee the trust, and nothing more. Revoke in one click.
